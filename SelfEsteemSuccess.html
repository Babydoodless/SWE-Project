WHAT IS SELF ESTEEM?

Self Esteem is a person's total sense of Self Confidence and Self Worth.

A healthy self-esteem promotes resilience, a positive self-perception, and improved decision-making. It is shaped through thoughts, experiences, and social factors.

What can help improve this is through self-awareness and constructive practices.


WHO IS IMPACTED BY LOW SELF ESTEEM?

Low self-esteem can affect anyone, no matter their age or background.

It’s especially common among teenagers, people dealing with stress, those who have been through trauma, or individuals struggling with mental health. It can also affect people who frequently compare themselves to others, perfectionists, or those in unhealthy relationships.

The main causes are often past experiences, negative thinking, or outside pressures, but getting help and becoming more aware of these issues can help improve self-esteem.
